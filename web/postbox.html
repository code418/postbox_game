How to claim it

  1. 1Install The Postbox Game and sign in.
  2. 2Walk to within 30 metres of this postbox. The circle above shows roughly where to look.
  3. 3Name its royal cypher to claim it. You can claim each postbox once a day.

What's the game?

Turn every walk into a treasure hunt.

Britain's red postboxes carry the royal cypher of the monarch who reigned when they were installed. Spot them on your walks, claim one a day for points, and chase the rare Victorian and Edward VII boxes up the leaderboards.
