The Postbox Game

Hunt Britain's royal postboxes

Turn every walk into a treasure hunt. Every red postbox carries the royal cypher of the monarch who reigned when it was installed. Spot them, claim one a day for points, and chase the rare ones up the leaderboards.

Find and claim

Walk up to any postbox in the UK, scan, and name its royal cypher to claim it. Each box can be claimed once a day, so there's always a reason to explore somewhere new.

Hints, not directions

A fuzzy compass shows roughly which way unclaimed postboxes lie, never their exact spot. You still have to go and look, which is half the fun.

Play with friends

Daily, weekly, monthly and all-time leaderboards. Add friends, filter the leaderboard to just them, and keep a daily streak going.

On your wrist

On Android, a standalone Wear OS app scans, follows the compass and claims from your watch, with a tile and watch-face complications for your streak and today's points.

Help keep the map right

Postbox data comes from OpenStreetMap. Report a missing postbox or a wrong cypher from the app, and accepted fixes update everyone's scores.
