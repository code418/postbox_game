How it works

Three steps. Some walking involved.

You have to be standing at a postbox to claim it, so the app gives you hints, never a pin on a map.

  1. 1

    Scan for nearby boxes

    Open the app on a walk. It counts the postboxes around you and points you roughly the right way. Never to the exact spot, so you still have to look.

  2. 2

    Name the royal cypher

    Stand within 30 metres of the box and pick the cypher on its front. EIIR, GR, VR... the letters tell you who was on the throne when it went in.

  3. 3

    Claim your points

    Each box can be claimed once a day. Rarer cyphers score more, and a claim every day keeps your streak alive.

Points

Rarer boxes, bigger scores.

Every postbox carries the cypher of the monarch on the throne when it went in. The rarer the cypher, the more it's worth.

  • EVIIIR 12 pts

    Edward VIII

    1936 · 143 mapped

    The rarest box in the game.

  • CIIIR 9 pts

    Charles III

    2022 on · fewer than 100 mapped

    The newest boxes, and still scarce.

  • EVIIR 9 pts

    Edward VII

    1901–1910 · about 2,700 mapped

    Edwardian, and easy to walk straight past.

  • VR 7 pts

    Victoria

    1837–1901 · about 3,600 mapped

    The oldest boxes you can claim.

  • GR 4 pts

    George V, George VI and the Scottish Crown

    GR, GVR, GVIR, crown · about 18,500 mapped

  • EIIR 2 pts

    Elizabeth II

    1952–2022 · about 36,500 mapped

    Probably the one at the end of your road.
