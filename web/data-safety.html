The Postbox Game – Data Safety

What data the App collects and how it is used.

Summary: The App collects your location (while in use only), your account display name and email, and gameplay activity. The position you claim from is kept for 90 days for anti-cheat checks, then removed automatically. Data is encrypted in transit. You can delete your account, or download a copy of your data, from within the App at any time. We do not sell your data.

Data Sharing

Question Answer
Is data shared with third parties? No – data is processed by Google Firebase (our infrastructure provider) on our behalf. The one exception: in Route Mode, the destination text you type is sent to OpenStreetMap's Nominatim search service. Your GPS position is never sent to it
Is data sold? No
Is data used for advertising? No
Is data used to track users across other apps? No

Security Practices

Practice Status

You can request data deletion Yes – delete your account and all associated personal data from within the App (Settings → Delete account)
You can download your data Yes – Settings → Privacy → "Download my data" returns a machine-readable copy of everything we store about you

Location Access

The App requests precise location permission. Location is accessed only while the App is in the foreground (i.e. not in the background). It is used to find postboxes near you and to validate that you are close enough to claim one.

The position you claimed from is stored with that claim so we can detect location spoofing, and is automatically removed after 90 days. Other players never see your location: nearby postboxes are shown only as fuzzy directions, never exact positions.

Data Retention

Data Retention
Claim location, timing and device token Removed automatically 90 days after the claim
Report photos and notes Removed 30 days after the report is reviewed
Anti-abuse anomaly records Deleted after 180 days
Account and gameplay history Kept while your account exists; removed or anonymised when you delete it

Account Deletion

You can delete your account and all associated personal data from within the App: Settings → Delete account. Deletion happens immediately. Your personal data is removed, your attached photos are deleted, and your past claims are anonymised so leaderboard history stays consistent without being linked to you.

If you can no longer sign in, contact us at richard@agilepixel.io and we will process the deletion within 30 days.
