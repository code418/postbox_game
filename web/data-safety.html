Summary

The App collects your location (while in use only), your account display name and email, and gameplay activity. The position you claim from is kept for 90 days for anti-cheat checks, then removed automatically. Data is encrypted in transit. You can delete your account, or download a copy of your data, from within the App at any time. We do not sell your data.

Data collected

  • Precise location

    Required

    GPS coordinates while using the App. The position you claimed from is stored with that claim, then removed after 90 days

    Why: Find nearby postboxes; validate claims; anti-cheat

  • Name

    Required

    Display name (chosen by you or from Google)

    Why: Shown on leaderboards and to friends

  • Email address

    Required

    Email used to sign in

    Why: Account authentication

  • App interactions

    Required

    Postbox claims, scores, streaks, leaderboard entries

    Why: Core gameplay features

  • Device token

    Required

    Random per-install identifier sent with claims (not derived from hardware); removed after 90 days

    Why: Multi-account abuse detection

    Optional

    Photos you choose to attach to a postbox problem report. These may include the time and place the photo was taken (EXIF). Removed 30 days after the report is reviewed

    Why: Evidence for reviewing reported postbox data problems

  • Problem reports

    Optional

    Report location, any note you write, and the cypher you suggest. Notes are removed 30 days after review

    Why: Correcting the postbox database and OpenStreetMap

  • Friends list

    Optional

    Why: Friend leaderboards and profiles

  • Crash logs

    Optional

    Anonymous crash reports and stack traces

    Why: Bug fixing and App stability

  • App performance

    Optional

    Anonymous performance traces (load times, etc.)

    Why: Performance improvements

  • Usage analytics

    Optional

    Usage events such as scans, claims and screens viewed (Firebase Analytics), linked to your account's user ID and never used for advertising — on by default; can be turned off in Settings → Privacy

    Why: Understand how the App is used

Location access

The App requests precise location permission. Location is accessed only while the App is in the foreground (i.e. not in the background). It is used to find postboxes near you and to validate that you are close enough to claim one.

The position you claimed from is stored with that claim so we can detect location spoofing, and is automatically removed after 90 days. Other players never see your location: nearby postboxes are shown only as fuzzy directions, never exact positions.

Data retention

  • 90 days

    Claim location, timing and device token

    Removed automatically 90 days after the claim

  • 30 days

    Report photos and notes

    Removed 30 days after the report is reviewed

  • 180 days

    Anti-abuse anomaly records

    Deleted after 180 days

  • Account life

    Account and gameplay history

    Kept while your account exists; removed or anonymised when you delete it

Account deletion

You can delete your account and all associated personal data from within the App: Settings → Delete account. Deletion happens immediately. Your personal data is removed, your attached photos are deleted, and your past claims are anonymised so leaderboard history stays consistent without being linked to you.

If you can no longer sign in, contact us at richard@agilepixel.io and we will process the deletion within 30 days.
